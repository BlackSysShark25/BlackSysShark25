## Hi, I'm Artem

I run a small fleet of Linux servers and home routers for people who need a connection that
keeps working when the network around them does not. Medicine is my day job; evenings go to
bash, iptables, WireGuard, Xray and reading tcpdump output.

What you will find here:

* [warp-relay](https://github.com/BlackSysShark25/warp-relay): a UDP relay for VPN servers
  that lives in the kernel (DNAT), with a watchdog and a small console. Every fix in it came
  from a real outage, not from a tutorial.

Things I spend time on: Keenetic and OpenWrt routers, split routing, AmneziaWG, Xray,
Cloudflare WARP, and the boring parts of keeping dozens of boxes alive (backups, sysctl,
conntrack, systemd ordering).

Russian is my first language, so issues and pull requests in either language are fine.
